Deploy Travel Canvas

After uploading the code-only template to a new public GitHub repository, paste that repository's URL here. This offline page opens the official Cloudflare deployment wizard. It never uploads your data.

Cloudflare creates the D1 database automatically. Before any travel plans are accessible, you must still enable Cloudflare Access for your email and enter the three Access identifiers in Worker settings. The app stays locked until you do.
